## English (en-US)

**App name** (up to 30 characters)
RaceFocus: reaction race

**Short description** (up to 80 characters)
Tap the numbers in order as fast as you can. Races, records, live delta.

**Full description** (up to 4000 characters)

RaceFocus is a Schulte table with a motorsport twist. The board is full of shuffled numbers and you tap them in order as fast as you can. One board is one lap. Every wrong tap costs a time penalty, so just like on track, the clean and quick lap wins.

🚦 A REAL START
Three red lights, green, go. The next laps start straight away, no pauses, like in a race.

📊 LIVE DELTA
A sim-racing style delta bar: green to the right while you gain, red to the left while you lose. Compared with your best lap number by number.

🏁 FLAGS AND PENALTIES
Yellow flag after a mistake, white before the final lap, chequered at the finish.

🏎️ RACE YOUR FRIENDS
Create a private room, send the link and race live on the same boards. Positions, gaps to the leader and the car ahead, results with the fastest lap.

🤖 RACE AGAINST BOTS
Up to 11 rivals, from easy to expert, or bots that drive at your pace. Works offline.

🏆 LEADERS
Track records with a podium for the fastest drivers.

⏱️ TRACKS AND MODES
Sprint 3×3, Street 4×4, Circuit 5×5, Endurance 7×7. Count up or count down. Race by laps or by time.

⛑️ YOUR HELMET
Design your helmet: colour, design and race number. Other drivers see it.

Train focus, reflexes and visual search in sessions of a few minutes. No ads during play. In English and Polish.
